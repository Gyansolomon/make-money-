# Channel plan

## Two channels
1. **History, Africa-focused.** GeoGlobeTales' storytelling style applied to stories he doesn't cover. Goal: "the GeoGlobeTales of Africa".
2. **Curiosity: "Unbelievable, but true."** Crazy true stories from money, war, crime, science and history. Every video needs a "wait, WHAT?!" moment.

Rules for picking topics:
- **Stakes, not trivia.** Money, lives or countries on the line; a person with a crazy plan or a huge mistake; a twist in the middle. "Why jeans have a small pocket" is a fact, not a story.
- **Not flooded online.** The Emu War, the Eiffel Tower con and the 38-minute war have been done to death.
- **African stories are our edge.** Almost no big channel covers them, and the creator is in Ghana.
- **Avoid:** news, politics, health advice (risky under YouTube rules), gossip.

## Competing with GeoGlobeTales (2M followers)
Same topics plus weaker edits means viewers pick him. So:
1. **Pick lanes he doesn't cover** (Africa, the Global South).
2. **Story first.** Viewers forgive simpler visuals if the story hooks them; they don't forgive boring stories.
3. **Close the edit gap step by step** (see `editing-upgrade-plan.md`).
4. **Volume:** big channels post 3–4 Shorts a week; the system can make one a day.
5. **Keep income separate:** the clipper (Whop content-reward campaigns) earns while the channels grow.
