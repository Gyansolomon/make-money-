### 7. Top 10 Production Techniques (Ranked)
1.  **Dynamic Map Movements:** Constant zooming and panning prevent the viewer from looking away.
2.  **Color Coding:** Using high-contrast colors (Yellow/Red/Pink) against a dark satellite map to instantly identify the subject.
3.  **Word-by-Word Captions:** Keeps the viewer's eyes locked on the center-bottom, increasing retention.
4.  **High-Frequency SFX:** Using "Whooshes" for every movement creates a tactile, high-energy feel.
5.  **Internet Culture Integration:** Using "Troll Face" memes to explain complex geopolitical rivalries (Sydney vs. Melbourne) makes the content relatable.
6.  **Mixed Media Overlays:** Placing stock photos in "Polaroid" style frames over the map to provide real-world context.
7.  **Fast Pacing:** No shot lasts longer than 3 seconds, catering to short attention spans.
8.  **Thematic Lighting:** Desaturating the map of Egypt (B&W) while keeping the country red creates a dramatic, serious tone for the final segment.
9.  **Visual Metaphors:** Using stick figures to represent population density on the Brazilian coast.
10. **Glitch Effects:** Using visual and audio glitches when discussing "military zones" or "protests" to subconsciously signal tension.
