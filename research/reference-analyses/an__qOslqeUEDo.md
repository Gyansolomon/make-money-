### 3. On-Screen Text & Captions Style
*   **Font:** Heavy, bold, sans-serif (similar to *Impact* or *Montserrat Bold*).

*   **Animation:** Word-by-word kinetic text that pops in sync with the narrator's voice.

### 7. Top 10 Production Techniques (Ranked)

1.  **OverSimplified Cartoon Aesthetic:** Using minimalist, expressive 2D character puppets to represent historical figures makes complex history lighthearted and highly watchable.
2.  **Dynamic Map-Based Storytelling:** Using high-contrast, color-coded maps with moving arrows to show troop movements keeps the geography clear.
3.  **Kinetic Word-by-Word Captions:** Large, high-contrast text popping up in sync with the voiceover maximizes viewer retention.
4.  **Humorous Dialogue Skits:** Breaking up the historical narration with comedic character interactions (like the "pasta" phone call) prevents viewer fatigue.
5.  **Countryball-Style Map Eyes:** Adding expressive cartoon eyes directly onto the country maps to show greed, fear, or sadness.
6.  **High-Contrast Color Coding:** Assigning bold, distinct colors to each nation (e.g., Germany = Purple, France = Yellow) so the viewer can instantly identify factions.
7.  **Rapid-Fire Pacing:** Keeping average shot durations under 3 seconds to match the fast-paced scrolling nature of YouTube Shorts.
8.  **Punctual Sound Effects:** Using cartoonish "pops," "dings," and "whooshes" to emphasize visual elements as they appear on screen.
9.  **The "Counter-Intuitive" Hook:** Starting with a statement that challenges common knowledge ("Hitler's biggest headache wasn't the USSR...") to immediately pique curiosity.
10. **Thematic Orchestral BGM:** Low-volume, dramatic military music that establishes the historical setting without competing with the narration.
